# Your legal guarantee rights

Everything you buy in this shop as a consumer has a legal guarantee. This is the European
Union's harmonised notice on the legal guarantee, as the European Commission
publishes it (Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1960).

## In Portugal

In Portugal, the legal guarantee lasts 3 years from delivery (Article 12 of
Decree-Law No 84/2021).

If a piece does not match what you ordered, write to
[{{EMAIL}}](mailto:{{EMAIL}}) with a photograph and the order number. What you
can choose — repair, replacement, a price reduction or a refund — and when is
set out in the [terms of sale](/legal/terms/).

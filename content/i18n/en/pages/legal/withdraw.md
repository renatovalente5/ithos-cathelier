# Withdraw from the contract

If you bought as a consumer, you can cancel the purchase here, on the site,
within the {{COOLING_OFF_DAYS}} days of your [right to cancel](/legal/cancellation/)
— European law calls it withdrawal. Fill in the statement and press
**Confirm withdrawal**: you get an acknowledgement of receipt by email, with
what you sent us and the date and time you sent it (Lisbon time).

## By email, if you prefer

You can also withdraw by email. Write to [{{EMAIL}}](mailto:{{EMAIL}}) with
your name, the order number (it starts with IC- and is in your confirmation
email), the email you ordered with and, if you are not cancelling the whole
order, which items. If the form does not appear on this page, use email: it
has the same effect. If you like, copy the
[cancellation form]({{FORM_URL}}) into your email.

## What counts

What counts is the date you send us your decision: if you send it before the
period ends, you have withdrawn in time (article 11 of Decree-Law 24/2014).
For a personalised piece, you can withdraw until we start making it; from then
on, this right no longer applies — see [what cannot be
cancelled](/legal/cancellation/).

We use what you write only to deal with your withdrawal and to send you the
acknowledgement of receipt. See [privacy](/legal/privacy/).

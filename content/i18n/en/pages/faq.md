# Questions

## How long does it take?

An in-stock lamp leaves the workshop in {{IN_STOCK_DAYS}}. If it is out of stock, you can still order it: we will make yours, and it will reach you in {{TO_ORDER_WEEKS}}. Each product page specifies which is the case.

cathelier pieces are always made to order — {{TO_ORDER_WEEKS}}, starting from the day you approve the design. If any piece in the order is made to order, everything will be sent together.

## Can I have a name on it?

Yes, on anything, and it costs nothing extra. Put it in the engraving box on the product page — a name, a date, or a short phrase.

One thing to know: a piece with a name on it is made for you alone, so it does not have the {{COOLING_OFF_DAYS}}-day right to cancel. Choosing a colour we already offer is not personalisation and does not affect your right to cancel.

## Is it safe in a baby’s room?

These are decorative night lights, not toys. They are made of solid pine with water-based paints and every edge is sanded by hand, but they are not tested as toys and should not be handed to a small child to play with.

If it is going in a cot room, keep the cable out of the cot. [Everything about care and safety](/care-and-safety/).

## Batteries or mains?

Both, at the same price. The battery version takes two AA cells, which are included. The mains version comes with a remote that dims it, times it and makes it pulse.

## What does delivery cost?

It depends where you are. [The full table is here](/legal/shipping-and-returns/).

## Can I change my mind?

You have {{COOLING_OFF_DAYS}} days to change your mind. A personalised piece is made just for you, so it does not have those days. [How it works](/legal/cancellation/).

## Do you take large orders?

Yes — favours, place cards, corporate gifts, club trophies. [Ask for a quote](/cathelier/quote/) and say roughly how many.

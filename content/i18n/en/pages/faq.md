# Questions

## How long does it take?

An in-stock lamp leaves the workshop in {{IN_STOCK_DAYS}}. If it is out of stock, you can still order it: we will make yours, and it will reach you in {{TO_ORDER_WEEKS}}. Each product page specifies which is the case.

cathelier pieces are always in stock: they leave the workshop in {{IN_STOCK_DAYS}}, counting from the day you approve the design. If any piece of the order is made to order, everything will be sent together.

## Can it include a name?

Yes, on any piece. For the lamps, engraving costs €2.50. For cathelier pieces, the name is already included in the price; for those that charge for extra letters, the product page will state the cost. Write it in the engraving box on the product page — a name, a date, or a short phrase.

One thing to note: a piece with a name is made just for you, so it does not include the {{COOLING_OFF_DAYS}} days right to cancel. Choosing a colour that we already offer is not personalisation and does not affect your right to cancel.

## Is it safe in a baby’s room?

These are decorative night lights, not toys. They are made of solid pine with water-based paints and every edge is sanded by hand, but they are not tested as toys and should not be handed to a small child to play with.

If it is going in a cot room, keep the cable out of the cot. [Everything about care and safety](/care-and-safety/).

## Batteries or mains?

Both, at the same price. The battery version takes two AA cells, which are included. The mains version comes with a remote that dims it, times it and makes it pulse.

## Can I cancel my purchase?

You have {{COOLING_OFF_DAYS}} days to cancel your purchase. A personalised piece is made just for you, so these days do not apply. [See the conditions](/legal/cancellation/).

## Do you accept large orders?

Yes — decorative pieces, keepsakes, gifts and trophies in wood, MDF, plywood and acrylic. [Request a quote](/cathelier/quote/) and let us know roughly how many.

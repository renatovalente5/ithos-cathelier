# Care and safety

## These are lights, not toys

An ithos piece is a decorative night light. It is not a toy, it is not tested as
one, and it should not be handed to a small child to play with.

## How to care for it

Clean it with a dry cloth. Never use water, never solvents, never anything abrasive — the paint is water-based and the wood is sealed, and both will suffer.

Keep it away from direct sunlight. Pine lightens after months of intense light, and the painted colours will also be affected by sun exposure.

If a light stops working, write to us. Do not open the piece, as it will not return to its original state.

## Repair parts

As the makers, we keep the parts needed to repair each piece available for 10
years after the last unit of that model is placed on the market (Article 21 of
Decree-Law No 84/2021). If it needs repairing, write to us.

## Batteries

The battery-powered versions require two AA batteries, which are already included. Remove them if the piece will not be used for several months — a leaking battery can damage the wood and the power system.

Keep loose batteries away from small children.

## The corded version

The corded version comes with a controller that regulates the light intensity, turns it on and off, includes a timer so it switches off by itself, and makes the light pulse slowly.

Keep the cord out of the cot and away from the bed. Do not pass it under a rug or behind a radiator.

## Wood moves

Solid pine responds to the room it lives in. In a very dry room a hairline may
open along the grain over a winter, and close again in summer. This is how
solid wood normally behaves. If in doubt, write to us: if you bought from us
as a consumer, the legal guarantee always applies.

# Stand-in photographs

These ten come from the owner's own Instagram (@cathellier) and are here **to
test with**. They are 361x640 reel covers, which is why the cathelier side
serves only 200px and 400px renditions: writing them out as "-1000" would tell
a browser to download a bigger file to see the same picture blurrier.

Forty-one pieces share ten photographs, assigned by what each one actually
shows rather than at random — an Easter favour gets the Easter photograph. The
owner replaces them one at a time in the back office.

Two of the twelve were dropped: one is a blurred video still, and one is a
koala night light, which belongs to ithos.
